# MLSC Events Journey

## What I’ll build
- A full-screen animated opening featuring the clear white MLSC-inspired dragon mascot and interactive glass waves.
- A black-and-white event roadmap with Microsoft-color accents, six milestones, hover motion, and a mascot that follows scroll progress.
- Individual pages for MLSC Inauguration, Tech Room, Praxis, Datathon, Code Sprint, and Bluebit Hackathon.
- Each event page will use animated headings, concise editable information, a photo gallery, and reserved footer space.
- The supplied photos will be grouped by event, including the newly uploaded Datathon set.

## Interaction and visual direction
- Crisp editorial typography, restrained glass surfaces, subtle moving belts, and purposeful motion rather than generic effects.
- Smooth roadmap curves and responsive layouts for phone and desktop.
- Reduced-motion support for accessibility.

## Technical details
- TanStack routes for the roadmap and each event detail page.
- Uploaded photos served through project assets; custom mascot variations generated with transparent backgrounds.
- Scroll-position tracking for mascot movement and CSS-driven entrance, hover, glass, and marquee effects.
- Unique page titles and social metadata for every event route.
